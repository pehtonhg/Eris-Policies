Eris Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026

This Policy explains how Eris, a Discord bot operated by Peh, processes information while providing its commands, games, profiles, economy, and related features.

1. Information Eris processes

  • Discord identifiers and account display information: user, server, channel, message, and interaction identifiers; usernames or display names; avatars; and membership or permission information needed to respond to commands, render cards, check access, and provide server-scoped features. Some display information is obtained from Discord when needed rather than stored as a separate permanent profile.
  • Player progress and activity: XP and levels, reputation, virtual currency balances, inventories and equipped cosmetics, pets and nicknames, achievements, badges, social links such as in-game partners or BFFs, purchases, gifts, trades, auctions, game statistics and outcomes, cooldowns, streaks, and timestamps. Related participant identifiers and transaction records may be retained to keep shared features consistent and prevent duplicate rewards or abuse.
  • Content you submit: custom profile text, titles and appearance settings, pet nicknames, reminder text and schedules, feature-specific submissions, and messages or information you send to the operator for support.
  • Messages and commands: Eris accesses message content available to it to recognize prefix commands, process feature inputs, and determine eligibility for chat rewards. Its XP duplicate-detection system stores hashes of recently processed text and activity timestamps, rather than an ordinary transcript of server conversations. Text deliberately supplied to features, such as reminders or profile biographies, is stored separately as described above. Hashes linked to a user are still treated as user-related data.
  • Server configuration and operational information: command prefixes, configured channels, access restrictions, active game or drop state, and diagnostic or error information needed to run and troubleshoot Eris.

2. How information is used

Information is used to provide requested features, maintain player progress, deliver reminders, produce profile and leaderboard displays, manage server configuration, enforce permissions and cooldowns, prevent exploitation and duplicate actions, investigate errors or abuse, and answer support or privacy requests.

Some player data is associated with a Discord user across servers. Leaving a server or removing Eris from a server does not automatically erase that shared progress.

3. Information visible to other users

Other users may see profile information, ranks, virtual balances, achievements, equipped items, in-game relationships, transaction participants, auctions, and game results through the corresponding features. Responses and reminders posted in a channel may be visible to anyone who can access that channel. A submission described as anonymous may still require the operator to process its author’s identifier; anonymity to other users does not necessarily mean anonymity to the operator.

Avoid putting passwords, payment details, health information, or other sensitive personal information into bot commands, profiles, reminders, or public feature submissions.

4. Storage and retention

Eris stores persistent records in the database and storage used to operate the bot. Recovery backups may also contain those records. The current automatic backup system retains up to seven recent successful snapshots; separate manual or recovery copies may also exist. This is a snapshot count, not a guarantee that every copy expires after seven days.

Progress and other records are kept while needed for their stated features, service integrity, support, or permitted security purposes. Some completed game and reminder records are not automatically removed immediately after use. Removing the bot from a server does not automatically delete all associated information. You can request deletion using the contact details below.

Following a verified deletion request, the operator will promptly delete or anonymize relevant personal data that is no longer permitted or required to be retained, including addressing retained backup copies. If limited retention is required by law or justified for a permitted security purpose, the operator will explain any applicable limitation. A restored backup must not be used to reinstate information covered by a completed deletion request.

5. Access and sharing

Data may be accessed by the operator and authorized people or service providers only as needed to operate, maintain, secure, or support Eris. Feature responses and interactions are transmitted through Discord. Information may also be disclosed when legally required or reasonably necessary to address abuse or protect users and the service. Eris does not sell personal data or use it for targeted advertising.

Discord handles its own processing under its Privacy Policy. These policy pages are intended to be hosted on GitHub Pages, whose hosting may process connection information under the GitHub Privacy Statement. The pages themselves add no analytics scripts, tracking cookies, or contact forms. GitHub Pages is not used to publish Eris’s player database.

6. Your choices and requests

You can stop using Eris, edit available profile settings, cancel pending reminders through the relevant commands, or ask a server administrator to change the bot’s permissions or remove it. These actions do not necessarily delete previously stored records.

To request access, correction, or deletion of your data, email pehtonhg@gmail.com or contact Discord username peh. Include your Discord user ID and what you want changed or deleted. For server configuration requests, include the server ID and your role there. The operator may need to verify that you control the relevant account or are authorized to act for the server. Do not send passwords or bot tokens.

Deleting data may remove progress, cancel pending actions, or affect shared game and social records. A request does not give access to another person’s private data. Information in messages already posted to Discord is also subject to Discord’s controls and the permissions of the relevant channel.

7. Security and age requirements

Access to operational storage should be limited to authorized operators. No system can guarantee complete security; report suspected exposure or unauthorized access using the contact details below.

Eris is intended for people permitted to use Discord under the minimum age requirement applicable in their country. If you believe personal data was collected from someone who is not permitted to use Discord, contact the operator so it can be investigated and addressed.

8. Policy updates

Changes will be posted here with an updated effective date. Material changes will be communicated through an appropriate bot or support announcement where required.
